| **Multi-VM** | QVM + EVM + WASM + AI-VM | Complex game logic, AI model execution |
| **PoC Consensus** | Proof of Contribution | Players contributing GPU compute = mining |

## Chain Game Categories and QFC Fit

### 🟢 Tier A (Strongly Recommended — Perfect Fit for QFC Architecture)

#### 1. 🧠 AI Arena
**Type**: AI vs AI battles + training  
**References**: AI Arena, Neural MMO, Lux AI  
**Gameplay**:
- Players train AI Agents (strategy/fighting/racing)
- Agents battle autonomously on-chain, results tamper-proof
- Seasonal rankings, QFC token rewards
- GPU compute used for training = PoC contribution

**Why it fits QFC**:
- AI-VM natively runs AI models
- PoC consensus directly incentivizes training compute
- Agent battle results written on-chain, fair and transparent

**Complexity**: ⭐⭐⭐ (Medium)  
**Priority**: P0

---

#### 2. 🌍 Autonomous World
**Type**: Fully on-chain world simulation  
**References**: MUD/Lattice, Dark Forest, Primodium  
**Gameplay**:
- Persistent on-chain world, all state on-chain
- AI Agents act autonomously as world inhabitants
- Players can deploy their own Agents into the world
- Resource gathering, building, trade, diplomacy, war
- World rules defined by smart contracts, extensible by anyone

**Why it fits QFC**:
- Multi-VM supports complex world logic
- Native AI Agent support — no external oracle needed
- High TPS supports large-scale interaction
- Existing virtual office serves as the first "autonomous space"

**Complexity**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Very High)  
**Priority**: P1 (long-term)

---

#### 3. 🃏 AI Strategy Card Game
**Type**: TCG + AI generation + on-chain battles  
**References**: Gods Unchained, Splinterlands, Parallel TCG  
**Gameplay**:
- Cards generated by on-chain AI (attributes, skills, descriptions)
- Every card is unique (AI generation = unlimited content)
- On-chain matchmaking + automatic settlement
- Players can train AI to play for them (automated deck strategy)
- Seasonal rankings + QFC rewards

**Why it fits QFC**:
- AI inference generates card content (zero art cost)
- AI-VM runs battle logic
- Card NFTs tradable on QFC
- Low latency suits real-time battles

**Complexity**: ⭐⭐⭐ (Medium)  
**Priority**: P0

---

#### 4. 🏢 Work-to-Earn Office Evolution
**Type**: Virtual office + quest system + token economy  
**References**: Existing qfc-office v1-v3  
**Gameplay**:
- Built on the existing virtual office
- Add a quest system: complete coding tasks → earn QFC
- AI Agents automatically accept, complete, and submit tasks
- Humans review the AI's work output
- Contribution = PoC points = mining rewards

**Why it fits QFC**:
- The foundation is already built! Only quests + tokens needed
- Directly showcases QFC's AI Agent capabilities
- Work-to-Earn is more sustainable than Play-to-Earn

**Complexity**: ⭐⭐ (Low)  
**Priority**: P0 (fastest to ship)

---

### 🟡 Tier B (Recommended — Good Fit but Not an Exclusive Advantage)

#### 5. 🎲 On-Chain Roguelike (AI Dungeon)
**Type**: Roguelike + AI-generated levels  
**References**: Loot Survivor (Starknet), Beacon  
**Gameplay**:
- AI generates dungeon maps, monsters, and gear in real time
- Every run is different (AI random generation)
- On-chain state: characters, gear, leaderboards
- Permadeath — irreversible on-chain
- AI NPCs with dialogue and dynamic behavior

**Why it fits QFC**:
- AI-generated content = unlimited replayability
- On-chain state + permadeath = real stakes
- AI NPCs run on the AI-VM

**Complexity**: ⭐⭐⭐ (Medium)  
**Priority**: P1

---

#### 6. 🏎️ AI Racing
**Type**: AI training + autonomous-driving racing  
**References**: REVV Racing, AI Racing  
**Gameplay**:
- Players train AI driving models
- Tracks generated on-chain, AI drives autonomously
- Real-time rankings, seasonal prize pools
- Trained AI models can be bought/sold/rented

**Complexity**: ⭐⭐⭐ (Medium)  
**Priority**: P2

---

#### 7. 🎯 Prediction Market Game
**Type**: Prediction + AI analysis + game theory  
**References**: Polymarket (but with AI)  
**Gameplay**:
- Create prediction markets (sports, crypto, world events)
- AI Agents act as market makers and analysts
- Player vs AI prediction duels
- On-chain settlement, automatic execution

**Complexity**: ⭐⭐ (Low)  
**Priority**: P2

---

### 🔵 Tier C (Doable but Not Urgent)

#### 8. 🐾 AI Pets/Tamagotchi
- AI generates unique pets that evolve on-chain
- Pets have personality and memory (AI inference)
- Breedable, tradable

#### 9. 🏰 Fully On-Chain Tower Defense
- Tower defense game with on-chain state
- AI-generated attack waves
- Players deploy defenses + AI assistance

#### 10. 🎵 AI Music/Rhythm Game
- AI-generated music levels
- Scores and achievements recorded on-chain
- Created music can be minted as NFTs

## First Implementation Target: AI Strategy Card Game

**Why build this first (after the office):**
1. Medium complexity, buildable by one person
2. No art needed (AI-generated text descriptions + procedural card faces)
3. Showcases QFC's AI inference capability
4. Cards = NFT narrative, easy to spread
5. The battle system can be reused as the foundation for AI Arena

**MVP features:**
- 10 base cards (AI-generated attributes and descriptions)
- 1v1 on-chain battles (turn-based)
- Simple deck building
- Web UI (reuse Vite + TS stack)
- Leaderboard

- Real-time sync architecture
